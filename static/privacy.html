Privacy

Last updated October 2026 · free public beta

Thought → Speak is a free beta. Features may change, and if we ever have to reset the database we'll say so on the site first and give you time to export your history.

What we store

  • Your email address, and your name if you give one.
  • Your password — only as a salted scrypt hash. We never store or see the password itself.
  • What you practise: your transcripts, the recordings you choose to save, your scores and coaching, and your lesson and streak progress.
  • One sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in. No advertising cookies, no trackers, no analytics.

Where your words go

  • Live transcript. The words that appear while you speak come from your browser's speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge that audio is processed by Google's or Microsoft's speech service, under their privacy policies. If you'd rather not use it, type or paste your answer instead.
  • AI coaching. To write your corrected answer and coaching, your transcript is sent to Groq, which runs Meta's open Llama models. When you record with the mic, the recording is also sent to Groq to be transcribed (more accurately than the browser). Groq doesn't keep these by default; it may log them for up to 30 days only to investigate errors or abuse (Groq's data policy). No name or email is sent with them. If AI coaching is busy or its free daily limit is reached, the built-in coach on our own server takes over and nothing leaves the server.
  • Coaching. Right now your answers are scored and coached by the built-in coach on our own server. No AI service receives your transcripts or recordings.

Who can see it

Only you, when you're signed in. Every take, transcript and recording belongs to your account and isn't shown to other users. The site's owner can technically reach the server to keep it running and to make backups, and doesn't look at people's practice. We don't sell or share your data.

Keeping and deleting

  • Delete a single take from History — its transcript, recording and scores go with it.
  • Download everything as a file with History → Export.
  • Delete your account under History → Delete account. That removes your account and everything in it. Nightly backups are kept for 7 days, so a deleted account is gone from backups within a week.
  • Trying it as a guest? Guest practice has no email attached and is deleted automatically after 14 days, unless you create an account (then it moves into your account). You can delete it sooner the same way.

Age

Thought → Speak is for people aged 13 and over.
